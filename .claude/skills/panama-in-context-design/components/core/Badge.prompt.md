Rounded-full pill for article taxonomies, categories, tags, and small status markers.

```jsx
<Badge variant="brand">History</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Canal</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline" size="sm">UNESCO</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Confirmed</Badge>
```

Variants: `brand` (brand-50 fill), `neutral` (gray fill), `outline` (faint ringed tag), plus `success | error | warning`. Sizes `sm | md`. `dot` prepends a status dot.
