Concepts

Reading the three cities

Historians often describe Panama City as three cities in one: the ruins of Panamá Viejo, the colonial quarter of Casco Antiguo, and the modern high-rise capital. Each layer marks a different chapter — and together they let us trace 500 years of change in a single afternoon's walk.

Key Terms

Camino Real
The "Royal Road" — the overland mule route that carried silver across the isthmus.
Privateer
A private sailor licensed by a government to attack enemy ships and ports.
UNESCO World Heritage
An international designation protecting sites of outstanding cultural value.

"Every site in Panama tells a story — our task is to learn how to read it."

— Diego Raúl Feliu, Panama In Context
