Casco Antiguo

UNESCO World Heritage

A city rebuilt behind walls

After 1673, Panama's capital rose again on a rocky peninsula ringed by defensive walls. Its narrow, cobblestoned streets blended Spanish colonial, French, and early republican architecture into the quarter we now call Casco Antiguo.

Today its plazas, churches, and the famous Golden Altar of San José make it an open-air classroom for Panama's colonial and independence history.
