Five Centuries

A timeline of Panamá's history

1519

Panamá Viejo is founded

Pedrarias Dávila establishes the first European settlement on the Pacific coast of the Americas.

1671

Morgan's raid destroys the city

The Welsh privateer sacks and burns Panamá Viejo; the capital is abandoned.

1673

Casco Antiguo is founded

The city is rebuilt two miles west on a fortified, defensible peninsula.

1821

Independence from Spain

Panama joins Gran Colombia, ending three centuries of Spanish rule.

1914

The Panama Canal opens

The waterway reshapes global trade and Panama's national identity.
